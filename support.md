# Support

**Pleats — a workbook for makers who sew**

Write to **support@plts.app**. I read every email and usually answer within two working days. In the app, Settings › "Send feedback" opens a draft with your app version and iPhone model already filled in, which helps me find the problem faster.

## Common questions

**Where is my stash stored?**
On your iPhone only. Pleats has no account and no sync. Your stash is included in your iPhone's backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if backups are turned on.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Open Settings › Recently Deleted. Deleted fabrics, notions, patterns and projects stay there for 30 days.

**How do I move my stash to a new iPhone?**
Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one. To keep a copy of your fabrics outside the phone, export them as a CSV file from Settings › Data (Pleats+).

**How do I cancel Pleats+?**
On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pleats, and tap Cancel Subscription. Pleats+ stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Cancelling a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends means you won't be charged.

**I bought Pleats+ but the app doesn't show it.**
In Pleats, open Settings and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple ID you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**How do I turn off usage statistics?**
Settings › About › "Share anonymous usage". See the Privacy Policy for what is sent.
