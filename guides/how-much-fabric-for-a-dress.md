# How much fabric do I need for a dress?

If you have the pattern, its yardage chart is the answer: find your size, your view and your fabric width. If you're buying without a pattern, or want to check whether a piece in your stash could work, you can estimate from your own measurements.

![A sewist fitting a slip dress on a dress form](/guides/img/dress-form.jpg)

## Rough amounts

These are starting points for an adult dress in a plain fabric, before any extra for nap, one-way prints or matching. Check them against your pattern.

- **Sleeveless shift or slip dress, knee length:** about 1.5–2 m of 140–150 cm wide fabric, or 2–2.5 m of 110–115 cm wide.
- **Dress with sleeves, knee length:** add about one sleeve length, often 0.5–0.7 m.
- **Gathered or full skirt:** the skirt alone can take two or three times the skirt length. A midi with a gathered skirt and sleeves is often 3–4 m of 140 cm fabric.
- **Maxi length:** add the extra skirt length once for every time the skirt pieces run down the fabric.

## Estimate it from your measurements

1. **Measure the finished length** from the shoulder to where the hem will fall, and add 10–15 cm for hem and seam allowances.
2. **Check whether the front and back fit side by side.** Lay out the widest point of the front and of the back (usually the hip or the hem, plus seam allowances). If both fit across the fabric's width, the body takes one length. If not, it takes two.
3. **Add the other pieces:** sleeves, facings, pockets, a collar. On wide fabric they often fit beside the body; on narrow fabric they usually need their own length.
4. **Add 10% for safety,** more for one-way designs or plaids.

Example: a knee-length shift is 95 cm from shoulder to hem. With 15 cm for hem and seams that's 1.1 m per length. On 150 cm fabric the front and back fit side by side: 1.1 m. Short sleeves add about 0.4 m: 1.5 m. With 10% extra: buy 1.65 m. On 115 cm fabric the front and back need a length each: 2.2 m plus 0.4 m for sleeves, about 2.85 m with the extra.

## Before you buy

- **Prewash, then measure.** Cotton and linen can lose several centimetres per metre in the first wash.
- **Check the width on the bolt,** not on the label you remember. It varies more than you'd think.
- **Measure what's already in your stash** first. A 2 m piece you already own may be enough for a sleeveless version.
