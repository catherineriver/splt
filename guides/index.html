Sewing guides

Plain answers to the questions that come up between the fabric shop and the finished make. From Pleats, the fabric stash app for iPhone.

Your stash

  • How to keep a fabric stash inventory: what to record for each fabric, how to keep it current, spreadsheet or app.
  • How much does fabric shrink? Typical first-wash shrinkage for cotton, linen, viscose, wool, silk and synthetics, and how to test a piece.

Planning a project

  • Do I have enough fabric for this pattern? Reading a yardage chart by size and width, and allowing for shrinkage, nap and matching.
  • How much fabric do I need for a dress? Rough amounts by style, and how to estimate from your own measurements.

Cost and price

  • How to work out what a sewing project cost: fabric by the length used, notions, the pattern and your hours, with a worked example.
  • How to price handmade clothes: covering materials, time and overheads, and choosing markup, margin or an hourly rate.
