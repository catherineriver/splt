# Pleats — fabric stash app for iPhone: sewing inventory, project planning and cost

Fabric stash app for iPhone · free during the beta

**Stop buying fabric you already own.** Pleats is a fabric stash organizer for people who sew. It keeps your fabric, patterns and notions on your iPhone. Check it in the shop before you buy, see whether a project is covered before you cut, and know what each make really cost when it's done.

- No account
- Your stash stays on your phone
- Metres or yards

## Sound familiar?

- **The same navy linen, bought twice.** It was on sale, it looked new, and the first one was in a box under the bed. Pleats checks your stash from the shop floor.
- **The pattern says 2.2 m. You have… some.** You find out it's short after the pattern pieces are already laid out. Pleats shows the gap before you start.
- **"What did that shirt cost you?"** Fabric, buttons, thread, the pattern, six evenings. Nobody adds it up. Pleats adds it up, hours included.

## Every fabric you own, with its length

Snap a photo and type the length and fiber. That's enough to start; width, weight, price and where it's stored can wait.

- **Check before you buy.** In the shop, point the camera at a bolt and Pleats tells you if something close is already at home.
- **Reserved fabric stays reserved.** Cloth set aside for a project stops counting as free.
- **Bring your spreadsheet.** Import a CSV and keep going.

## Know if the stash covers it before you cut

Pick a pattern and its yardage fills in what the project needs. Assign fabric from your stash, and Pleats shows what's covered and what's still missing.

- **The gap, in metres.** "1.5 of 2.2 m" means you know exactly what to buy.
- **Patterns remember their yardage.** Type it once and the next project fills in.

## See what a make really cost

Fabric is priced by the length you used, not the whole cut. Notions, the pattern and your hours go on top, and nothing missing is quietly counted as free.

- **Log time as you go,** by hand or with a timer on the Lock Screen.
- **Selling what you make?** Pleats suggests a price, with profit, margin and your hourly rate.

## Your stash is yours

Fabrics, photos, prices and notes stay on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Pleats sends only anonymous counts of which features get used, and you can turn that off in Settings.

## Questions

- **Do I need an account?** No. Pleats works on your iPhone without signing up, and your stash never leaves it.
- **What happens to my data if I change phones?** Your stash is part of your normal iPhone backup, through iCloud Backup or your computer. Restore the backup and it comes with you. Pleats has no sync of its own yet.
- **Metres or yards? Dollars or euros?** Either. You pick units and currency when you set up the app, and you can change them in Settings.
- **What's free after the beta?** The free plan keeps up to 30 fabrics, 10 notions, 5 patterns and 3 active projects, with the shop check, planning and the total cost of each project. Pleats+ lifts the limits and adds the line-by-line cost, sale price, timer, stats and export.
- **I already keep my stash in a spreadsheet. Why switch?** Import the spreadsheet as CSV and keep it. What a spreadsheet can't do: check a bolt against your stash from the shop with the camera, hold fabric reserved for a project so it stops counting as free, and add up a make from the length you actually used.
- **Is it on Android or iPad?** Not for now. Pleats is made for iPhone.
