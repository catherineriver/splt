Know what’s on your fabric shelf, and what a make really costs.

Pleat keeps your fabric, patterns and notions in one place. When you plan a project, it shows which fabric you already have enough of. When you finish, it adds up fabric, notions, the pattern and your hours.

Your stash, with lengths

Photograph a fabric and type its length and fiber. Width, weight, where it’s stored and what you paid can wait until you have a minute.

Already keep a spreadsheet? Import it as CSV.

Check before you buy

In the shop, point the camera at the bolt. Pleat compares its color, and any details you set, with what you own, and tells you if something close is already at home.

Does the stash cover it?

Pick a pattern, and its yardage fills in what the project needs. Each piece shows the fabric you’ve assigned, or how much is still missing.

Fabric set aside for a project stops counting as free, so you don’t promise the same linen twice.

What it cost to make

Fabric is priced by the length you used, not the whole cut. Notions, the pattern and the hours you logged go on top.

If a fabric has no price, Pleat says so instead of counting it as free. From the total it can suggest a sale price, if you sell what you make.
