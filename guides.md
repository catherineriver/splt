# Sewing guides

Plain answers to the questions that come up between the fabric shop and the finished make. From Pleats, the fabric stash app for iPhone.

## Your stash

- ![Shelves of folded fabric in a fabric shop](/guides/img/fabric-shelf.jpg) [How to keep a fabric stash inventory](https://plts.app/guides/fabric-stash-inventory): what to record for each fabric, how to keep it current, spreadsheet or app.
- ![Washed cotton and linen drying on a clothesline](/guides/img/sheets-on-line.jpg) [How much does fabric shrink?](https://plts.app/guides/fabric-shrinkage) Typical first-wash shrinkage for cotton, linen, viscose, wool, silk and synthetics, and how to test a piece.

## Planning a project

- ![Hands cutting out a paper sewing pattern with scissors](/guides/img/cutting-pattern.jpg) [Do I have enough fabric for this pattern?](https://plts.app/guides/enough-fabric-for-a-pattern) Reading a yardage chart by size and width, and allowing for shrinkage, nap and matching.
- ![A sewist fitting a slip dress on a dress form](/guides/img/dress-form.jpg) [How much fabric do I need for a dress?](https://plts.app/guides/how-much-fabric-for-a-dress) Rough amounts by style, and how to estimate from your own measurements.

## Cost and price

- ![Buttons, thread and a safety pin on a table](/guides/img/buttons-thread.jpg) [How to work out what a sewing project cost](https://plts.app/guides/sewing-project-cost): fabric by the length used, notions, the pattern and your hours, with a worked example.
- ![Handmade dresses on a wooden clothes rack](/guides/img/dress-rack.jpg) [How to price handmade clothes](https://plts.app/guides/pricing-handmade-clothes): covering materials, time and overheads, and choosing markup, margin or an hourly rate.
