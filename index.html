Your stash

Every fabric you own, with its length.

Snap a photo and type the length and fiber. That’s enough to start; width, weight, price and where it’s stored can wait.

  • Check before you buy. In the shop, point the camera at a bolt and Pleat tells you if something close is already at home.
  • Reserved fabric stays reserved. Cloth set aside for a project stops counting as free.
  • Bring your spreadsheet. Import a CSV and keep going.

Planning

Know if the stash covers it before you cut.

Pick a pattern and its yardage fills in what the project needs. Assign fabric from your stash, and Pleat shows what’s covered and what’s still missing.

  • The gap, in metres. “1.5 of 2.2 m” means you know exactly what to buy.
  • Patterns remember their yardage. Type it once and the next project fills in.

Cost

See what a make really cost.

Fabric is priced by the length you used, not the whole cut. Notions, the pattern and your hours go on top, and nothing missing is quietly counted as free.

  • Log time as you go, by hand or with a timer on the Lock Screen.
  • Selling what you make? Pleat suggests a price, with profit, margin and your hourly rate.

Your stash is yours.

Fabrics, photos, prices and notes stay on your iPhone. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking. Pleat sends only anonymous counts of which features get used, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need an account?

No. Pleat works on your iPhone without signing up, and your stash never leaves it.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Your stash is part of your normal iPhone backup, through iCloud Backup or your computer. Restore the backup and it comes with you. Pleat has no sync of its own yet.

Metres or yards? Dollars or euros?

Either. You pick units and currency when you set up the app, and you can change them in Settings.

What’s free after the beta?

The free plan keeps up to 30 fabrics, 10 notions, 5 patterns and 3 active projects, with the shop check, planning and the total cost of each project. Pleat+ lifts the limits and adds the line-by-line cost, sale price, timer, stats and export.

I already keep my stash in a spreadsheet. Why switch?

Import the spreadsheet as CSV and keep it. What a spreadsheet can’t do: check a bolt against your stash from the shop with the camera, hold fabric reserved for a project so it stops counting as free, and add up a make from the length you actually used.

Is it on Android or iPad?

Not for now. Pleat is made for iPhone.
