# Privacy Policy

**Pleat — a workbook for makers who sew**
Effective date: 19 October 2026

Pleat is made by Ekaterina Baliasnikova, an independent developer in Riga, Latvia ("I", "me"). I am the controller of the personal data described here. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Pleat app for iPhone and the website plts.app.

The short version: **Pleat keeps your stash on your iPhone.** There is no Pleat account, no advertising and no tracking. I don't receive your fabrics, patterns, projects, photos, prices or notes. The app sends anonymous usage counts (which features get used) so I can see what to fix, and you can turn that off in Settings.

## 1. What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter in Pleat stays on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

- fabrics, notions, patterns and projects, with their lengths, widths, prices, notes and tags
- photos you take or pick for them, and pattern PDFs you import
- time you log on projects, journal entries, and your settings (units, currency, hourly rate)

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. It's included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you have backups turned on, under Apple's terms. Pleat has no sync of its own.

## 2. Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera.** Used only when you take a photo of a fabric, notion or project, or scan fabric in a shop. Photos are saved inside Pleat on your iPhone.
- **Photos.** Pleat uses Apple's photo picker, so it only ever sees the photos you choose. It doesn't get access to your library.
- **Files.** When you import a pattern PDF or a CSV file, Pleat reads only the file you pick.
- **Live Activities.** When you run the labour timer, it can show on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. This stays on your iPhone.

You can say no to any of these. The rest of the app keeps working.

## 3. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features makers use and where they get stuck, Pleat sends short usage events, such as "a fabric was added from the camera", "a project was created", "the cost breakdown was opened" or "the paywall was shown from the fabric limit".

- **What an event carries:** the event's name; categories and counts (for example how a fabric was added, or a rough stash size like "6–20 fabrics"); and technical details added by the analytics service: app version, iOS version, iPhone model, language and region settings.
- **What it never carries:** fabric, pattern or project names, prices, notes, photos, colours, links, files, your email, or any advertising identifier. The app can't attach these: an event has no slot for text you typed.
- **Who receives it:** TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany), which processes the events on my behalf and stores them on servers in Germany and the Netherlands. To tell installs apart without knowing who you are, the service uses a random identifier that is hashed on the device and again on the server, so neither I nor TelemetryDeck can link it to you, your Apple ID or your other apps. TelemetryDeck never stores your IP address. See telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
- **How to turn it off:** Settings › About › "Share anonymous usage". When it's off, nothing is sent, and nothing is saved up to be sent later.

Legal basis (GDPR): my legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it, using anonymous data only (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by turning sharing off.

## 4. When the app goes online

Apart from the usage statistics above, Pleat only connects to the internet when you ask it to:

- **Importing from a link.** When you paste a link to a fabric or pattern page, your iPhone loads that page (and its cover image) directly from that shop's website to read the details. The shop sees an ordinary request from your iPhone, as it would in a browser, identified as "Pleat". I don't see the link or the page. What the shop does with that request is covered by the shop's own privacy policy.
- **Reading pages on the device.** On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Pleat may use Apple's on-device model to read a pasted page or text. This runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to me.
- **Purchases.** If you buy Pleat+, payment is handled entirely by Apple. I receive no card or account details from Apple, only whether a purchase or free trial is active, which Pleat checks on your iPhone. Apple sends me sales reports that don't identify you.

## 5. When you choose to share

Some actions send data out of Pleat because you started them: exporting your stash or a project's costs as a CSV file, or sharing a file through the iOS share sheet. Where that data goes is up to you, and is covered by the app or service you send it to.

**Send feedback.** In Settings, "Send feedback" opens a draft in your mail app. It includes a short summary you can read and edit before sending: app version and build, iOS version, iPhone model, and how many fabrics, patterns and projects you have and how many have a complete cost. Nothing is sent until you send the email. I receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply and to fix what you report.

**Storage recovery.** If Pleat ever can't open its storage, it keeps the old data aside instead of deleting it and shows you a notice. Its "Send file" button only shares that file if you choose to, for example to send it to me for help recovering your data. I use such a file only to recover your data, and delete it once that's done.

## 6. The website and the mailing list

plts.app is a plain website with no analytics or advertising cookies. If you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to my own sign-up service and kept so I can send you news about Pleat and beta invites (about one email a month). The website and the sign-up service run on Cloudflare, which processes the address on my behalf. I don't sell or share the list. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to me, and I'll delete your address.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 7. What I don't do

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. (If you've turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with me under Apple's own policy.)
- No tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifier, and no data brokers.
- I don't sell or rent personal information.

## 8. How long I keep things

- Your stash: on your iPhone until you delete it. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and are then removed. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone (but not from any backups you've made).
- Usage events: anonymous, so they can't be traced back to you. TelemetryDeck keeps them in long-term storage and expects to delete them after 7–10 years.
- Feedback and support emails: kept as long as needed to answer and follow up, and no longer than 2 years after our last exchange.
- Mailing-list address: until you unsubscribe.

## 9. Your rights

Because your stash never leaves your iPhone, you're fully in control of it: you can view, change, export or delete anything in the app at any time.

For anything I do hold, such as your mailing-list address or emails you send me, you have the right to access it, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent. Usage events are anonymous, so I can't find the ones from your iPhone; turning sharing off stops new ones. Write to me at the address below, and I'll answer within one month. If you're in the EU or EEA and think I've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv) or your local data-protection authority.

**California residents:** I don't sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, I don't use it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and you won't be treated differently for using any of your rights.

**India:** for questions or grievances under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, write to the address below.

## 10. Children

Pleat isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU), and I don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, write to me and I'll delete it.

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, I'll say so in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
